Client Report

I decide to make a site about video games. I chose this topic because it's something a know a lot about and I enjoy learning more about. I chose images that I believe would help support the rest of the content on the site. I tried communicating with my developer through Teams, but he did not respond to my messages. My developerdid not meet my expectations for my site. He has not added any of the content I provide, resulting in a mostly empty site with some lorem ipsum and stock images. Next time I hire a team to do a project for me, I will make sure they are reliable, and will frequently communicate to me with updates.

The Gamer's Guide

Lead Developer Report

As the lead developer, I tried to stay ahead on the site, and had frequent communication with my junior developer so he would have plenty of time to work on the child pages. communication with my client went really well. We used Teams to communicate and would frequently send updates to each other as the site developed. I used GitHub to publish the site, and gave the junior developer access to edit it. I then sent the link to the site to the client so he could view the progress as we worked on it. Managing the site on GitHub was fairly simple. I didn't run into any major issues with hosting the site.
